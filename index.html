What is DOT?

Directly Observed Therapy (DOT) is the World Health Organization's recommended strategy for treating tuberculosis. A trained health worker observes the patient swallow every dose of TB medicine — ensuring the treatment is completed and drug resistance is prevented.

  • ✔ Treatment is completely free at government DOT clinics
  • ✔ Standard treatment lasts 6 months
  • ✔ TB is curable with proper treatment
  • ✔ All information is kept confidential

If you have a cough lasting more than 2 weeks, fever, night sweats, or unexplained weight loss, please visit the nearest DOT clinic listed on this site.

🏥

TB Symptoms

  • Persistent cough (>2 weeks)
  • Coughing up blood
  • Chest pain
  • Night sweats
  • Unexplained weight loss
  • Fatigue & fever
